Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our packing jobs

Is there any registration fee?

Yes, a one-time fee of ₹620 is required. This covers material handling, training, and your identity verification. The fee is fully refundable after you complete your first 100 units.

How much can I earn per month?

It depends on how many units you pack. On average, workers earn between ₹30,000 and ₹50,000 per month. The more you work, the more you earn.

Do I need a computer or special equipment?

No. All you need is a basic smartphone for communication and training. We provide all packing materials—boxes, wrappers, etc.—to your doorstep.

How are payments made?

We transfer payments directly to your bank account or UPI (PhonePe, Google Pay) every week after quality inspection.

Can I work part-time?

Absolutely. You can choose your own hours—morning, evening, or night. The work is flexible and can be done around your other commitments.
